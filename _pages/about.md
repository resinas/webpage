My research is about helping organisations and people understand and improve how work gets done. I work at the intersection of business process management (BPM), process mining and information systems, combining conceptual work (notations, languages and patterns) with data-driven analysis and tools that people can actually use. You can find a more detailed description of each line on the [research](research/) page:

- **[Process performance measurement](research/#performance-measurement)**: defining, computing and making sense of process performance indicators, from the PPINOT notation to natural language and large language models.
- **[The process of process mining and visual analytics](research/#process-of-process-mining)**: understanding how analysts carry out process mining and building visual tools that support them.
- **[Personal productivity and collaborative work](research/#productivity-collaboration)**: studying how people organise their work and collaborate with digital tools, using the data traces those tools leave.
- **[AI and conversational agents in BPM](research/#ai-conversational-bpm)**: using chatbots and large language models to automate decisions and support process analysis.
- **[Predictive process monitoring](research/#predictive-monitoring)**: predicting the outcome and performance of running processes, with predictions people can trust.
- **[Compliance management](research/#compliance-management)**: checking that processes comply with regulations and policies through flexible, low-code approaches.

Earlier in my career, I worked on the automated negotiation and analysis of service level agreements, and on the management of human resources in business processes.

My research is regularly published in journals like IEEE Transactions on Services Computing, Information Systems or BISE, and conferences like BPM or CAiSE. You can find the full list [here](publications/). I have been general chair and PC chair of the [BPM conference](https://bpm-conference.org/), and I'm currently associate editor in the [BISE journal](https://www.bise-journal.com/).

I am constantly looking out for great students to do the Bachelor Thesis (Trabajo Fin de Grado), Master Thesis (Trabajo Fin de Máster) or PhD Thesis (Tesis Doctoral). If you are a student at the University of Seville and like conversational interfaces, large language models, personal productivity, or process management, you might want to contact me to do a thesis with us.
